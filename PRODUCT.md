# Product

## Register
product

## Users
People seeking spiritual guidance, reflection, or entertainment through tarot card readings.

## Product Purpose
A digital tarot application that provides interactive tarot readings. It guides users through selecting a topic, choosing a spread, shuffling and drawing cards, and receiving interpretations in Thai language.

## Brand Personality
Mystical, intuitive, premium, calm, and insightful.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered, generic SaaS templates
- Overly bright, playful, or "childish" UI
- Overwhelming blocks of unformatted text
- Flat designs without depth or atmosphere

## Design Principles
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Use layered backgrounds, glows, and soft shadows to create a liminal, mystical space.
- **Intentional Pacing:** Ensure transitions between phases (topic -> spread -> draw -> reading) feel deliberate and ceremonious.
- **Typographic Authority:** Use elegant serif fonts for headings to convey ancient wisdom, paired with clean sans-serif for readable body text.
- **Tactile Mysticism:** Elements should feel somewhat physical, like premium card stock or foil accents, rather than purely digital.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Ensure text contrast is sufficient despite dark/mystical themes.
- Provide reduced-motion fallbacks for card shuffling and drawing animations.
